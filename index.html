About

Meet Tamari Machiya

Tamari Machiya is a retired teacher with a Diploma in Teaching, a Degree in Teaching and 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa. She brings a lifelong passion for educating the African child to her tutorials in Chitungwiza.
