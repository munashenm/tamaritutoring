About

Meet Tamari Machiya

  • Diploma in Teaching
  • Degree in Teaching
  • 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa

Tamari Machiya is a retired teacher with a Diploma in Teaching, a Degree in Teaching and 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa. She brings a lifelong passion for educating the African child to her tutorials in Chitungwiza. Her goal is to help learners strengthen their understanding and gain confidence in the classroom.

Subjects

Tutorial Series

Tutorials for learners in Grades 4, 5, 6 and 7. Enquire about one subject or all three.

  • Mathematics

  • Combined Science
