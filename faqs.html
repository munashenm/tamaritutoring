FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Answers from the details published for Tamari Tutorial Service. If your question is not here, send it from the contact page.

Who leads the tutorials?

Tamari Machiya, a retired teacher with a Diploma in Teaching, a Degree in Teaching and 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa. Read more on the About page.

Which grades do you teach?

Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6 and Grade 7.

Which subjects are offered?

Mathematics, English and Combined Science. You can enquire about one subject or all three.

Where are the tutorials?

Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe. A street address is not published on this website.

How do I enrol my child?

Email admin@tamaritutoring.com with the parent or guardian’s name, the learner’s grade and the subjects of interest. The enrolment section opens that message in your email app.

Are fees or lesson times listed?

No. Fees and lesson times are not published on this website. Ask about them by email.

Is there a phone number?

Yes. Call +263 782 712 631. The number is also on the contact page.

How do parents sign in?

Parents Login (opens in a new tab) opens the parent login on SchoolHub.
