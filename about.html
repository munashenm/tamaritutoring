About

About Tamari Tutorial Service

Grade 4 to Grade 7 tutorials in Mathematics, English and Combined Science, based in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe. The service is led by retired teacher Tamari Machiya.

Meet Tamari Machiya

  • Diploma in Teaching
  • Degree in Teaching
  • 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa

Tamari Machiya is a retired teacher with a Diploma in Teaching, a Degree in Teaching and 42 years of teaching experience across Southern Africa. She brings a lifelong passion for educating the African child to her tutorials in Chitungwiza. Her goal is to help learners strengthen their understanding and gain confidence in the classroom.

Tamari Tutorial Service is where that work continues. The tutorials follow the subjects learners meet at school: Mathematics, English and Combined Science, from Grade 4 through to Grade 7.

The tutorials

A place for learners in the foundation years to practise, ask questions and grow more sure of their work.

  • Who the tutorials are for

    Learners in Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6 and Grade 7.

  • Where

    Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe.

  • Subjects

    Mathematics, English and Combined Science. Enquire about one subject or all three.

  • How to enquire

    Email admin@tamaritutoring.com with the parent or guardian’s name, the learner’s grade and the subjects of interest. Parents who already have an account can use Parents Login (opens in a new tab).

What the tutorials cover

  • Mathematics

    Build confidence with numbers, practise calculations and learn how to work through problems step by step.

  • English

    Develop reading comprehension, strengthen grammar and practise expressing ideas clearly in writing.

  • Combined Science

    Explore science concepts and connect what learners study in class to everyday life.
