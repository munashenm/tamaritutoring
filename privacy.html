Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to information you send through the Tamari Tutorial Service website.

Who we are

Tamari Tutorial Service offers Grade 4 to Grade 7 tutorials in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe. The email address for this website is admin@tamaritutoring.com.

What you can send

The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. The newsletter box asks for an email address. The Become a Tutor form asks for your name, email address and a message about your teaching.

How it is delivered

Those forms are sent through FormSubmit, which delivers them by email to admin@tamaritutoring.com. The first time a form is used, FormSubmit emails that address an activation link. Messages are delivered after the link is opened.

We use what you send to reply to an enquiry, an enrolment question, a newsletter signup or a tutor enquiry. We do not sell this information.

Parents Login

Parents Login (opens in a new tab) opens a separate SchoolHub page. That page is not part of this website, and this privacy policy does not cover it.

This website

This website does not ask you to create an account. It does not publish fees, lesson times or a street address. The phone number is on the contact page.

Asking for a change

Email admin@tamaritutoring.com if you want a message you sent through this website corrected or deleted.

Our mission

To help learners strengthen their understanding and gain confidence in the classroom.
